Our story

A seaside kitchen built around one fire.

Ondessa began with a simple idea: the best coastal food is cooked close to the water and close to the flame. Everything on our menu passes through the hearth — smoke, char and patience doing most of the work.

  • Day-boat seafoodFish arrives the morning it's landed and is broken down in-house.
  • Oak-fired hearthNo gas burners on the line — just live fire, cast iron and embers.
  • Gulf Coast growersProduce from small farms within a two-hour drive, menus that follow the season.
