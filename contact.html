Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — the bar and patio are held for walk-ins every night. For the dining room, a reservation is recommended on weekends.

Is there a dress code?

Coastal casual. Come as you are.

Can you accommodate allergies?

Most dishes can be adapted. Let us know in your reservation notes and tell your server on arrival.

Is the restaurant accessible?

Our dining room, patio and restrooms are step-free, and we keep a printed large-type menu at the host stand.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes — gift cards are available at the host stand in any amount and never expire.
